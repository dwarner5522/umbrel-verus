Faster first sync

Verifying the whole chain from the first block can take days. A bootstrap downloads a recent copy of the blockchain published by the Verus project (about 25 GB) and continues syncing from there.

This replaces any blockchain data already downloaded. Your wallet is not touched.

PBaaS chains

Run other chains of the Verus network next to VRSC and merge mine them: the same hashes that mine VRSC through this node also mine every chain switched on here, at no extra cost to your miners. Each chain is a full node of its own that needs memory and disk space while it runs.

A chain switched on for the first time starts from a bootstrap: a recent copy of its blockchain from bootstrap.verus.expert, checked against the signature of its publisher before it is installed. Chains sync one at a time and run at a lower priority than the rest of your Umbrel.
